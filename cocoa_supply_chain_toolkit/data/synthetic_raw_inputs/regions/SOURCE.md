# Source of `departments.geojson`

The 108 department boundaries of Côte d'Ivoire in this folder are built from
the sub-prefecture (ADM3) boundaries published by geoBoundaries
(https://www.geoboundaries.org, dataset `CIV-ADM3`, gbOpen release), merged
into departments. The original source is the Comité National de Télédétection
et d'Information Géographique (CNTIG) and OCHA ROWCA, via the Humanitarian Data
Exchange.

Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 IGO (CC BY 3.0 IGO).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/

Changes: sub-prefectures were merged into departments, and the department
codes and names (`LVL_*_CODE`, `LVL_*_NAME`) were added so that they match the
codes used in the rest of the example data. The codes are identifiers for the
example only.

Citation: Runfola, D. et al. (2020) geoBoundaries: A global database of
political administrative boundaries. PLoS ONE 15(4): e0231866.
